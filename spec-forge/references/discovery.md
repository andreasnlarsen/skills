# Requirements Discovery

The root skill owns stage selection, runtime and authority. Discovery does not add an approval checkpoint.

For a requested interview, use [question-bank.md](question-bank.md) to choose material missing answers. Ask one focused question or a useful related group. Skip settled and retrievable answers; explicit delegation means choose within scope rather than return the same options.

For supplied context or delegation, draft from the brief, files and existing plan, including technical constraints that affect a prototype. With little context, propose an initial interpretation and ask only the gaps that could invalidate the next artifact. Keep facts separate from delegated choices and unknown business facts.

Preserve requirement identity and scope. Record material assumptions and their basis in the existing draft; DEFAULTED/TBD labels are optional. Reconcile corrections with dependent requirements and keep unrelated authorized work moving. Do not require a conversation-mode choice or a question quota.

Use [mece-thinking](../../mece-thinking/SKILL.md) when overlapping states, mixed-level categories or broken decision trees prevent a trustworthy specification. Otherwise finish when the current stage is decision-complete under its relevant [validation criteria](validation-checklist.md).
